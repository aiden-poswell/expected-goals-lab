## 7. Unresolved issues

- Sequence evidence for the suspect positions is mixed; the cause (a frame reversal over part of a sequence, wrong tagging, or something else) is not established, and shots with a wrong frame at ordinary-looking positions cannot be detected from coordinates alone.
- One match has a goal that is tagged only on the opponent goalkeeper's event; it is quarantined, not repaired.
- Regular shots are not guaranteed to be open play: they may follow set pieces.
- Pitch size, goal position and the x = 100 convention rest on documentation and the penalty-spot check; per-stadium dimensions are unknown.
- Provider tagging is human and unaudited; one season, five leagues, no provider xG.
- Nothing here has been split, fitted or evaluated.

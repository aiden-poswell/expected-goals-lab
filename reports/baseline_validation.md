## 1. Findings supported by the validation data

On the 5509 validation shots (587 goals, 257 matches, 2018-02-26 to 2018-04-13), after fitting on earlier matches only:

- Constant training rate: log loss 0.3393, Brier 0.09521.
- Distance only: log loss 0.2950, Brier 0.08545, AUC 0.761.
- Distance plus angle: log loss 0.2930, Brier 0.08468, AUC 0.763.
- Distance only versus constant, log loss: -0.0443 [-0.0510, -0.0375]: distance-only was better; the interval is entirely below zero.
- Distance plus angle versus constant, log loss: -0.0462 [-0.0535, -0.0388]: distance-plus-angle was better; the interval is entirely below zero.
- Distance plus angle versus distance only, log loss: -0.0019 [-0.0041, +0.0002]: no clear difference on these validation matches; the interval includes zero; Brier -0.00078 [-0.00153, -0.00001]: adding angle was better; the interval is entirely below zero.

Summary on angle: the two scoring rules disagree about whether adding angle is distinguishable from no change; only the Brier score interval excludes zero, so treat the evidence for angle as weak. Distance alone gives 96% of the log-loss gain over the constant baseline.

Intervals are 95% percentile intervals of a match-cluster bootstrap (1000 resamples, seed 20180513). They describe sensitivity to which validation matches were drawn. They are not guarantees for future matches or seasons, and there is one season of data. Nothing here concerns the test split.

|---|---|---|---|
| distance_only | distance_m | -1.2088 | -0.14666 |
| distance_only | intercept | -2.6231 | +0.1043 |
| distance_plus_angle | distance_m | -0.7030 | -0.08530 |
| distance_plus_angle | angle_rad | +0.3805 | +1.50097 |
| distance_plus_angle | intercept | -2.5298 | -1.5638 |

Direction check: farther shots should score less often (negative distance coefficient) and wider angles should score more often (positive angle coefficient). Observed: distance negative in the distance-only model; distance negative and angle positive in the two-feature model. Distance and angle are strongly related (both come from position), so the split of credit between them in the two-feature model should not be read as separate effects.

## 7. Limits and departures

- One season (2017/18), five leagues. Validation is a single contiguous window of 26 match dates; it supports a statement about those matches, not about other seasons.
- Teams and players appear in train and validation, as they would in a within-season forecast.
- Distances and angles assume a 105 x 68 m pitch; positions are integer percentages.
- Labels come from provider tagging (tag 101 on the shot event). 36 rows quarantined by S02 never enter.
- Only distance and angle were used. No body part, context, time or identifiers.
- Departures from `docs/BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`: none to the models, split, metrics or thresholds. A summary sentence on angle was added to this report after the first validation run (wording only; see the protocol, section 9).

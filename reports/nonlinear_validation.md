## 7. Limits

- One season (2017/18) and one contiguous validation window; no claim about other seasons, leagues or club-grade accuracy.
- `body_part_class` is a coarse provider class. `head_or_body` mixes headers with other body parts, so any difference associated with it is a statement about that tag, not about headers.
- Splines add flexibility (six columns per feature) with a fixed penalty; no tuning was done and none is implied to be optimal.
- Distances and angles assume a 105 x 68 m pitch.
- Departures from `docs/NONLINEAR_PROTOCOL.md`: none to the data, split, candidates, metrics, comparisons, reading rule, bootstrap or sensitivity. Two reporting additions were made after the first run: the sentence flagging readings that change in the goal-line sensitivity, and the table of fitted body-part terms (protocol, section 12).

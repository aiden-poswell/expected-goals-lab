## 6. Coordinates

Wyscout API documentation (support.wyscout.com/matches-wyid-events): `x`, `y` are integer "percentage from the left corner of the attacking team". The paper adds that x is nearness to the opponent's goal. Checks on the data:

- Penalties in play, all files (582): mean start position x = 89.3, y = 50.0; most common [((89, 50), 131), ((90, 50), 109), ((89, 49), 99)]. The penalty mark is 11 m from the goal line (Laws of the Game), so x near 89-90 implies a pitch length of about 103 m *if* the scale is linear and the mark is at 11 m.

With x < 10: 13 shots in 6 matches (2500923 (Rennes - Guingamp, 0 - 1), 2516742 (Freiburg - Eintracht Frankfurt, 0 - 0), 2516958 (Borussia M'gladbach - Werder Bremen, 2 - 2), 2516989 (Hannover 96 - RB Leipzig, 2 - 3), 2565753 (M\u00e1laga - Girona, 0 - 0), 2565775 (Sevilla - Girona, 1 - 0)).
